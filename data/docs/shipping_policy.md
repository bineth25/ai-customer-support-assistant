# ShopLite Shipping Policy

## Where ShopLite ships
ShopLite ships to the United States and Canada only. ShopLite does not currently ship to any other country.

## US shipping options and prices
- Standard shipping in the US takes 5 to 7 business days and costs $4.99. It is free on orders of $50 or more.
- Express shipping in the US takes 2 to 3 business days and costs $12.99. Express shipping is never free.

## Canada shipping options and prices
- Standard shipping to Canada takes 7 to 10 business days and costs $9.99.
- Express shipping to Canada takes 4 to 5 business days and costs $19.99.
- Free shipping does not apply to orders going to Canada.

## When delivery times start
ShopLite delivery times are counted from the day the order ships, not the day it is placed. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## Order processing time
ShopLite orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day are processed the same day. Orders placed later, on weekends, or on public holidays are processed the next business day.

## Order statuses
- Processing: the order is received and is being prepared. It has not shipped yet.
- Shipped: the order has left the warehouse and is with the carrier.
- Delivered: the carrier has delivered the order.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled before shipping.
- Returned: the customer returned the order and ShopLite received it.

## Tracking your order
When your ShopLite order ships, you receive an email with a tracking number. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear. You can also see the status in My Account > Orders.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address of a ShopLite order only while its status is "Processing". After the order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## Late or lost packages
If your ShopLite package has not arrived 5 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support. ShopLite will open an investigation with the carrier. If the package is confirmed lost, you can choose a replacement or a full refund.
