# ShopLite Frequently Asked Questions

## What does ShopLite sell?
ShopLite sells electronics accessories, home goods and clothing.

## How do I create a ShopLite account?
Click "Sign up" at the top of the ShopLite website and enter your email address and a password. You will receive a confirmation email.

## I forgot my ShopLite password. What do I do?
Click "Forgot password" on the login page. ShopLite emails you a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes.

## How do I find my order number?
ShopLite order numbers start with "SL-" followed by 4 digits, for example SL-1001. You can find yours in your order confirmation email and in My Account > Orders.

## How do I contact ShopLite support?
You can chat with the ShopLite support assistant or email support@shoplite.example. Human support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. ShopLite does not offer phone support.

## Can I change my ShopLite order after placing it?
Items in an order cannot be edited. To change an order, cancel it while its status is "Processing" and place a new order.

## Does ShopLite send order updates by email?
Yes. ShopLite sends an email when your order is placed, when it ships, and when it is delivered.

## Is my payment information safe with ShopLite?
ShopLite does not store full card numbers. Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider.
