# ShopLite Returns Policy

## Return window
ShopLite accepts returns within 21 days of the delivery date. The 21 days start on the day your order is marked "Delivered", not the day you placed the order. After 21 days ShopLite cannot accept a return, except for defective electronics covered by the warranty (see "Warranty" below).

## Condition of returned items
A returned item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all tags and accessories.

## Items that cannot be returned
ShopLite cannot accept returns for these items:
- In-ear earbuds once the seal has been broken (for hygiene reasons)
- Personalised or custom-engraved items
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page

## Return shipping fee
When you return an item because you changed your mind, ShopLite deducts a flat $4.99 return label fee from your refund. Return shipping is free when the item is defective, damaged, or the wrong item was sent.

## How to start a return
1. Log in to your ShopLite account and go to My Account > Orders.
2. Click "Start return" next to the order.
3. Choose the item and the reason for the return.
4. ShopLite emails you a prepaid return label. The label is valid for 10 days.
5. Pack the item, attach the label, and drop it off with the carrier.

## Damaged or defective items
If your ShopLite item arrives damaged or defective, report it within 7 days of delivery and include a photo. ShopLite will send a replacement or give a refund, whichever you choose. Return shipping is free in this case.

## Warranty
ShopLite electronics have a 12-month limited warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. The warranty does not cover accidental damage or water damage. To make a warranty claim, contact ShopLite support with your order number.

## Exchanges
ShopLite does not offer direct exchanges. To get a different item, return the original for a refund and place a new order.
