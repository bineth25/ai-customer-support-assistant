# ShopLite Payments and Refunds

## Accepted payment methods
ShopLite accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. ShopLite does not accept cash on delivery, bank transfers or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged
ShopLite charges your payment method when you place the order.

## Refund timing
After ShopLite receives your returned item, it is inspected within 2 business days. Once the return is approved, the refund is sent within 5 to 7 business days. Your bank may need extra time to show the money in your account.

## Where refunds go
ShopLite refunds only to the original payment method. ShopLite cannot refund to a different card or payment method.

## What is refunded
For a return, ShopLite refunds the price of the item. Original shipping charges are not refunded, unless the return is because of ShopLite's error (a defective item, a damaged item, or the wrong item). The $4.99 return label fee is deducted when you return an item because you changed your mind.

## Partial refunds
If a returned item shows signs of use or is missing parts, ShopLite may refund only part of the price. ShopLite emails you before issuing a partial refund.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel a ShopLite order free of charge while its status is "Processing", and you receive a full refund including shipping. Once the status is "Shipped", the order cannot be cancelled. You can return it after it is delivered.

## Promo codes
Only one promo code can be used per ShopLite order. If you return an item bought with a promo code, the refund is the amount you actually paid after the discount.

## Duplicate charges
If you see a duplicate charge for a ShopLite order, contact support with your order number. ShopLite investigates within 3 business days.
